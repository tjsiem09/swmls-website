💡 Tip for performing detailed searches

Every field shown in an MLS Public Detail Report corresponds to a search filter you can add using the “Add a Field” button. This makes it easy to match MLS terminology and fine-tune your searches to discover more listings with the features and amenities that matter most to you.

Map-based search

1. Nosy Neighbor
  • Click the blue info icon in the lower right corner of the map to activate the ‘Nosy Neighbor’ feature.
  • Pan/zoom to find a property on the map.
  • Click on any property to view past sales prices and listing history (if available).
2. Map overlays
  • Click 'Overlays' in the upper right corner of the map.
  • Select MLS areas, ZIP codes, school districts, zoning, or additional overlays to narrow down your preferred area.
